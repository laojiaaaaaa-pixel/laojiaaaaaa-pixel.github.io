AI-Generated News and Social Media Trust

How does AI participation in news production shape perceived human oversight, verification, trust, and user behavior?

The project uses OASIS to place heterogeneous simulated agents in controlled news environments. In Phase 01, nine fixed accurate-news texts were balanced across four production-mode disclosures, with 100 matched agents per mode and 400 complete first-exposure observations.

Paired false-news experiment

The same 25 false-news items were presented with and without an AI-generated disclosure label across news exposure, peer discussion, and expert correction. Belief in the false claim was lower in the labeled condition at all three stages.

−25.6 ppT1 label gap
−33.7 ppT2 label gap
−28.8 ppT3 label gap

All findings are descriptive simulation evidence and should be interpreted as mechanism signals, not causal estimates for real users.

02 · 2026–present

Power Politics and Normative Dynamics in China’s Territorial Disputes

Building a longitudinal database of diplomatic activity from multi-country news.

The project combines rule-based extraction and BERT classification to identify actors, institutions, visit direction, event type, and topic. The workflow emphasizes entity normalization, annotation consistency, and reproducible data-quality checks.
