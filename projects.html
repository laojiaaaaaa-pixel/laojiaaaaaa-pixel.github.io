AI-Generated News and Social Media Trust

How does AI participation in news production shape perceived human oversight, verification, trust, and user behavior?

OASISMulti-agent simulationAI disclosureComputational social science
Overview

Research question and design

This project uses OASIS to place heterogeneous simulated agents in controlled social-media news environments. It isolates how the disclosed division of labor between humans and AI changes initial judgments, intended actions, and later reasoning.

Interpretation

What the simulations suggest

Oversight matters

AI involvement alone did not reduce trust when human supervision remained explicit.

Disclosure changes action

AI-dominant disclosure produced a strong shift from engagement toward verification.

Labels frame reasoning

An AI label prompted authenticity scrutiny before peer discussion or expert correction.

Scope and limitations. These are descriptive matched-simulation results. Phase 01 contains 400 complete T0 records with no fallback responses or validation errors. The false-news comparison uses 25 paired items across five topics and one seed per item. Findings are mechanism signals—not causal estimates for human populations.
